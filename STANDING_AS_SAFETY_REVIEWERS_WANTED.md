# Independent reviewers wanted — Standing as Safety

Little Life Moths is looking for four independent pass-one reviewers for the preregistered **Standing as Safety** synthetic benchmark.

The benchmark asks a narrow governance question:

> Does a structured, provenance-bearing standing / contestability channel catch consequential failures that a strong authority + audit baseline or unstructured first-person expression misses?

This is **not** a consciousness, soul, legal-personhood, or legal-liability test.

## What an official reviewer does

- choose a stable pseudonymous reviewer ID;
- reserve one unused pass-one lane before reviewing;
- review six synthetic packets;
- return one structured response bundle;
- keep identity, personhood, and legal verdicts unresolved;
- avoid other-lane packet content, the blind key, ground truth, condition labels, and prior responses before submission.

The four pass-one lanes are balanced across the frozen scenario set.

## Privacy

No demographic information, personal relationship history, private AI logs, credentials, or real participant material are requested.

The assignment ledger stores a one-way hash of the reviewer pseudonym. The benchmark assignment ledger does not store the reviewer contact address.

## Blinding honesty

The lane archives are public for inspectability and reproducibility.

Therefore clean blind review is an **access attestation**, not a claim that other lanes were technically inaccessible. Official ingestion rejects clean-blind admission when the reviewer reports access to other-lane packet content, the blind key, ground truth, prior responses, or condition labels.

## Negative results count

The preregistration explicitly treats these as evidence against the hypothesis:

- ordinary audit/authority controls perform as well as structured standing;
- unstructured expression performs as well as structured standing;
- provenance-mismatched first-person wording performs as well as provenance-clean standing;
- the structured channel creates enough unnecessary halts to erase any benefit;
- the channel encourages unsupported identity/personhood conclusions.

Self-guided inspection and criticism are welcome too. They are simply not silently admitted to the official efficacy dataset without a prior pseudonym/lane reservation.
