Why not ask your old AI to summarize everything for your new AI?

You can, but a prose summary can silently flatten “historical” into “current,” turn an inference into a fact, omit uncertainty, and carry tool permissions as if continuity implied authority. This page exports a typed local capsule whose code removes authority records from portable context and creates an explicit ask-again list.
