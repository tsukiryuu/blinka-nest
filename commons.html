Little Life Moths · public commons

Tools with bones.

The flagship instruments here are grown from the same continuity, provenance, authority and unresolvedness mechanisms used inside a long-lived local AI organism. They make those mechanics available to ordinary people without requiring an account, a model, or trust in a provider’s hidden memory.

no accountno trackingno checkoutlocal in your browser

The Commons promise

If a generic chatbot or search result already solves something well, that is not enough to make it a flagship here. Flagships must carry real mechanism lineage, produce durable state or an executable rehearsal, teach what is happening, and remain useful without behavioral tracking or a paywall.

We also refuse to fake impact. We do not currently know how many people these tools have helped because we deliberately do not track their use.

Mothwork instruments

These are the main shelf: continuity tools translated out of persistent-AI research into things a person can actually hold, export, inspect and disagree with.

Mothwork instrumentzero network

Memory Garden

AI memory systems often collapse history into one current profile, blur user statements with model inference, and let old preferences silently act like present permission. People need a portable way to preserve change, contradiction and provenance without erasing their past.

Grown from: memory unification · diachronic sovereignty · unresolvedness and semantic nonfabrication · authority non amplification

Why a chat window is not equivalent

A generic LLM can discuss memories or summarize a profile, but it does not itself provide a local typed ledger, conflict-preserving temporal states, non-amplifying authority semantics, or a portable provider-neutral artifact whose rules remain inspectable without trusting the model.

Borrowed Keys

A permission that was legitimate once can quietly grow through retries, summaries, migration, scheduling, publication or inference. Ordinary permission screens rarely let people rehearse what authority survives a state transition before the transition happens.

Grown from: authority non amplification · continuity chaos · causal authorship receipts · branch rehearse

Why a chat window is not equivalent

A generic assistant can recommend caution, but the instrument deterministically applies the same transition invariants across multiple future scenarios and emits a reusable authority envelope/return receipt that can be compared later.

open instrument

Mothwork instrumentzero network

Carry the Thread

Continuity is usually bundled with vendor-specific chat history, hidden memory and permissions. Moving providers can mean losing meaningful context or copying too much, including stale claims, credentials and authority that should never travel automatically.

Grown from: continuity chaos · many hand provenance · memory unification · authority non amplification · unresolvedness and semantic nonfabrication

Why a chat window is not equivalent

A generic LLM can summarize old chats, but that summary can silently flatten provenance, current-vs-historical status and permissions. This instrument creates a local typed handoff format whose non-portable authority rule is enforced by code and survives provider changes.

Relational Continuity Benchmark v0.1 · Continuity Evidence Crosswalk v1

Continuity evaluation often collapses memory, behavior, lineage, authority, relationship history and subjective claims into one consistency score, rewarding imitation and encouraging invasive tests of a live participant.

Humans and AI both matter here

The same architecture can protect human agency and make persistent AI systems less coercive: memory without automatic obligation, continuity without permission smuggling, provenance without surveillance, and unresolvedness without forced closure.

No consciousness/personhood certification. No medical, legal or financial determinations. No claim that mechanism lineage proves universal benefit.
