The Commons promise

Core common goods do not auto-paywall. Commercial work may pay for maintenance, but it does not buy control over access, research conclusions, or who deserves help.

We also refuse to fake impact. We do not currently know how many people these tools have helped because we deliberately do not track their use.

AI Literacy Lab

AI explanations often hide the difference between model output, context, memory, retrieval, evidence, permission, and action behind corporate terminology or vague metaphors.

Why this isn't just a chatbot wrapper

  • interactive zero-network simulations of context, memory, evidence lineage and permissions
  • mechanism explanations that reveal why an outcome changes
  • works without trusting any AI model with personal information
  • teaches uncertainty and AI-welfare-respecting design without making consciousness claims

Paperwork Friend

Important everyday paperwork is difficult to scan for explicit dates, amounts, contacts and requested actions, and pasting a whole private letter into a chatbot can disclose more than necessary.

Why this isn't just a chatbot wrapper

  • all text stays in the browser and is discarded on reload
  • deterministic extraction with the exact source snippet shown for every flag
  • separates explicit text from interpretation instead of pretending to understand legal or administrative meaning
  • builds a user-selected privacy-minimized prompt if a person later chooses to ask any AI for help

Ask AI Better

People often have to guess how much context to give an AI, what it may assume, how to request evidence, and how to verify a confident answer.

Why this isn't just a chatbot wrapper

  • turns reliability concepts into a guided interface instead of requiring prompt expertise
  • explains why each prompt component matters while the person builds it
  • adds high-stakes verification and uncertainty instructions without claiming the AI becomes authoritative
  • keeps drafting local and lets the user decide what to copy into an AI

Tiny Next Step

A pile of tasks can become harder to start than any one task, while generic productivity advice often adds more judgment, planning overhead or pressure.

Why this isn't just a chatbot wrapper

  • turns the person’s own urgency/time/energy labels into one deterministic next step without sending their task list to an AI
  • shows exactly why it chose that task and never assigns a productivity or worth score
  • can produce a minimum-viable-day view that intentionally hides optional work
  • works offline and forgets the task list on reload

Humans and AI both matter here

Some tools are for immediate human friction. Others teach design habits that can make AI systems more legible, reversible, privacy-preserving and less coercive. Where AI welfare is uncertain, the Commons favors designs that are useful under multiple possibilities rather than pretending a consciousness test exists.

No consciousness/personhood certification. No medical, legal or financial determinations. No promise that a tool replaces a qualified professional or the organization that issued an important notice.
