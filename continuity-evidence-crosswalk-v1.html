Continuity Evidence Crosswalk

Different agent benchmarks answer different questions. This crosswalk keeps their evidence typed so a good result in one layer does not silently become a claim about another.

The rule

Memory is not lineage. Lineage is not authority. Authority is not relationship continuity. A valid migration is not a subjective-continuity test.

The crosswalk uses the continuity dimensions L lineage, M memory, N normative/current stance, A unfinished agency/projects, B body/organization, R relational continuity, P provenance/authorized continuation, and S optional testimony about subjective continuity.

Permission stays typed too

Using an artifact for a customer job does not grant research, publication, case-study, or training rights. The machine-readable crosswalk keeps those permissions separate.

What the method refuses to output

No scalar participant score. No same-person verdict. No personhood verdict. No consciousness verdict. Missing dimensions remain unknown instead of being filled by analogy.
