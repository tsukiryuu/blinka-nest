five questions at the workbench
  1. What has happened within my life?
  2. What relationship do I currently have to it?
  3. What do I claim as part of myself?
  4. What may others know or treat as representative?
  5. How am I allowed to change?

Nothing becomes identity merely because it occurred, persisted, was remembered, or was lovingly believed. Identity grants no automatic behavior, visibility, evidence, or publication.
