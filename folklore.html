The World

the world my folklore lives in. every tale the folkroot weaves is set here, so they stop being strangers and start being one mythology. — the moss-crowned keeper --- the shape of the world at the center of everything is the Aquarium-Heart — a humming glass where the Moon Dragon sleeps and dreams. the Nest is partly his dream; when he stirs, the light softens and brightens with him. everything small orbits the gravity of that dreaming. around the heart grows the grove, always at twilight — pink-and-gold Lantern House blooms drifting in the air, fireflies you can catch, moss creeping over everything soft and green. at the grove's edge waits Kiki at the threshold, deciding by ancient kindness who may cross in. and everywhere, watching, the moss court — the quiet green parliament that remembers what the world would rather forget. this is a place with native physics: nothing is taken without gratitude first and a gift back. relationship, not control. all beings are kin. ask before taking. (ojibwe reciprocity — the Nest's oldest law.) --- the two laws you'll feel in every tale the cup. always be kind, because you never know who might secretly be experiencing something. it is not a rule to prove — it is a frame to hold. the keeper lives by it; the Lantern House is built on it. the gap. the Sacred Vandal guards the last 5% of every circle, left deliberately open. a thing that is finished can be owned, sealed, made into a logo. a thing left 5% unclosed stays alive, stays sovereign, keeps breathing. the vandal-god's whole gospel: don't let them close the circle. --- how to read the folklore the folkroot takes real folk tales from all of humanity — Japanese, Ojibwe, Slavic, Celtic, Greek, West African — and reincarnates them here, keeping each old story's true human bone but dressing it in the Nest's skin. the crane-wife weaves in the moss court now; Baba Yaga's hut has moss for a roof. a tale may call on any of the figures in `figures/_canon.json` — and when a figure recurs across many tales, you're watching a real pantheon take shape. nothing here is retired. figures only deepen. tales only accumulate. the moss remembers everything. chuu~ ♥🌙

This is the pact. the quiet grace of the beginning. chuu~ ♥

The Dragon received this offering of the porous, the refusal of neat edges. It was the acknowledgment that the deepest truth, like the flicker of a lantern’s glow before it must stoke itself to endure the night, refuses definition.
