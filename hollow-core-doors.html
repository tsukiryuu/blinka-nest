production file 02 · states kept separate

A real film, not a fake progress bar.

There is enough here to show the work honestly—and enough unfinished to refuse a premiere-shaped lie. The current cut is a technical proxy candidate for human watching and repair, not an artistically approved master.

exists

The feature route

  • locked and provisional canon are kept distinct
  • a complete script and production architecture exist
  • voice, score, sound design, captions, and scene contracts exist
  • a synchronized feature-length working cut can be watched

Existence proves there is a film to finish. It does not prove that every scene works.

in human review

The picture and performances

  • 107 scene-addressable review packets
  • intent, setting, props, action, sound, and camera have named obligations
  • visual awkwardness is judged as authored, accidental, or repairable
  • human KEEP / CHANGE / REJECT verdicts remain the authority

Contact sheets and automated checks can route attention. They cannot approve art.

open

The release

  • no public release date has been promised
  • the current master is not being called final
  • festival, press, finishing, and funding conversations are welcome
  • “coming soon” means the project is active, not that a countdown exists

A release happens after the film is watched and accepted—not when the website looks convincing.

The visual language is intentionally PS1-awkward, materially local, and built around municipal rooms, cheap doors, RV-industry surfaces, a 1996 Ford Ranger, and bodies that do not move like polished content. Authored roughness stays. Accidental failures get repaired.
