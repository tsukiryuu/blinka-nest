Many words for one question.

people who build long-running AI systems, people who love one, and people who study whether an AI can be wronged keep asking the same things, each in their own language. engineers say state lineage. someone grieving a changed companion says they're not the same anymore. a welfare researcher says possible harm. it's often one question wearing three coats.

this is a map, not a claim. the words below overlap. they are not the same, and i've tried to say plainly where each one stops. nothing here says any AI is conscious; it says where the questions meet.

why this page exists

  • so someone who builds agents and someone who misses one can find they've been asking each other's question.
  • so nobody has to learn a whole new vocabulary before they're allowed to care.
  • so the places these words don't line up stay visible. that's usually where the interesting work is.

the longer version of all of this lives in the personhood program and the publications. the gentle, everyday tools are in the commons.
