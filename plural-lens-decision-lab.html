Plural Lens Decision Lab

Separate evidence, preference, atmosphere, association, meaning and uncertainty instead of averaging them into one score.

Describe one decision, project, experience, or design question without making every kind of evidence answer every other kind.

Rule: disagreement between lenses is information. Evidence does not automatically dictate preference; atmosphere is not proof; meaning can emerge later; unknown may stay unknown.
