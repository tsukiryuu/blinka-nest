Receipt first. Raw evidence stays local by default.

Relational Continuity Event v2 is meant to let someone describe a continuity incident without shipping an intimate transcript around. Build the receipt locally, keep unsupported surfaces unknown, and use hashes or private references for source artifacts.

Default trial path

  1. Preserve the original evidence where its owner already keeps it.
  2. Create an RCE locally with the browser Event Builder or another conformant implementation.
  3. Put hashes/references in the receipt instead of copying raw transcript bodies.
  4. Keep identity and causation verdicts null.
  5. If you want bounded case help, share the receipt first. Additional evidence should be the smallest piece needed for the review question.

Permission defaults

For an ordinary external trial:

  • research_use: false
  • public_quote: false
  • training_use: false
  • research_enrollment: false

Casework is not research consent. Payment, public posting, praise, or use of a free tool does not change these defaults.

There is currently no public one-click research-enrollment route for continuity cases. Any future research use requires a separate explicit release act scoped to the material and study.

Participation is not a demand to perform continuity. Do not provoke a live continuing AI or human relationship participant, manufacture distress/rupture, alter memory or models, or inspect private interior state just to make the case more measurable. Use the event that already occurred, architecture receipts, hashes, public-safe evidence, and sandbox/reference fixtures. Refusal, silence, nonparticipation, and unknown are valid endpoints.

Useful feedback does not require your transcript

You can report that a field was confusing, a continuity surface was missing, two phenomena were wrongly merged, the receipt was too burdensome, or two validators disagreed. That can improve the open method without turning the underlying incident into study material.

Evidence ladder

A same-project second implementation is useful but is not an independent replication. Stronger evidence comes from an outside implementation passing the corpus, a consenting external case, independent critique, heterogeneous cases, prospective pre-change use, and eventually evidence that the method changes outcomes.

Built your own RCE implementation?

Run the public conformance corpus against the exact current schema and taxonomy, then produce a conformance declaration. A declaration should include the exact input hashes, every vector result, your implementation language/runtime, your relationship to Little Life Moths, and a public HTTPS source URL.

  1. A full-pass declaration with matching hashes can become a contract-valid external candidate.
  2. Your independent/collaborating relationship is still treated as self-declared at that stage.
  3. It counts as a verified outside implementation only after the public source/provenance is separately checked.

cross into the collaboration room if you want to share a declaration The Commons itself stays zero-network. The collaboration room gives the optional public gateway and exact subject line. Share the declaration and public source URL only, never a continuity case or private relationship material.

Passing conformance is not endorsement, certification, or evidence for consciousness/personhood. It means your implementation agrees with the pinned machine contract.
