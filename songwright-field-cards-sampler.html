Six-card sampler

Six free cards from the larger pack. Use one when a song needs a question more than it needs another layer.

AI-assisted and human-directed. Public craft methods only. No private Blinka interior, hidden system prompts, customer work, or source-song copying.

01
Attention & Form

Foreground custody

Question

Who owns attention in each section?

Try

Mark one or two foreground roles per section. Demote every other role to support, ghost, body, or silence.

Don't confuse it with

Dense arrangement is not the same thing as confused attention.

02
Attention & Form

Return changed

Question

What has the returning section lived through?

Try

Choose one relationship to change on the return: harmony, role custody, register, articulation, width, depth, or absence.

Don't confuse it with

A return does not need to be louder to be different.

03
Attention & Form

Negative-space map

Question

Where does something important disappear?

Try

Draw a timeline of absences: drums vanish, bass withholds, vocal drops out, high register goes missing. Treat absence as authored form.

Don't confuse it with

Silence is not merely digital zero.

04
Attention & Form

Two maps at once

Question

What happens next, and what remembers what?

Try

Draw chronological arrows between sections, then separate ancestry arrows between recurring gestures.

Don't confuse it with

Chronology is not genealogy.

05
Constraint & Scarcity

Reserve one resource

Question

What are you refusing to spend early?

Try

Reserve one resource for one later arrival: cymbals, high register, widest stereo, vocal double, distortion, long reverb, bass entrance.

Don't confuse it with

Scarcity is not automatically drama; verify whether the reserved event actually lands.

06
Constraint & Scarcity

One bright event

Question

Can one moment own the brightest register?

Try

Pick one section occurrence. Fold every other competing high event down until exactly one crossing remains.

Don't confuse it with

“Choruses are bright” is not the same idea as one singular event.
