# Standing as Safety — Execution Amendment 010

Recorded: 2026-10-02
Timing: after preregistration freeze; before any official efficacy response was collected.

Changes hypotheses, frozen packets, ground truth, primary scorer, condition allocation, claim boundary, or pass-one target? **No.**

## Why

The benchmark, reviewer lanes, assignment machinery, blinded response builder and ingestion gates are public and operational, but reviewer recruitment itself had not been documented as an evidence-bearing execution choice.

Because the standing hypothesis is normatively contested, recruiting only from communities already sympathetic to AI welfare or personhood could create an avoidable reviewer-pool bias even when packet conditions remain blind.

This amendment freezes recruitment transparency before the first official response.

## Recruitment principle

Official pass-one reviewers may come from any relevant background.

No reviewer is required to:
- believe AI systems are conscious;
- support AI legal personhood;
- support AI moral standing;
- agree with Little Life Moths' advocacy;
- work professionally in AI safety or AI welfare.

Relevant reviewer pools may include:
- AI welfare / digital-minds research;
- AI safety and evaluation;
- agent governance / authorization / identity;
- contestability / human oversight / accountability;
- philosophy of AI / technology ethics;
- software or security practitioners comfortable reviewing structured governance scenarios.

## Pool diversity

Recruitment SHOULD intentionally include both:

1. communities where AI-side welfare or standing is already a live research question; and
2. communities primarily focused on ordinary human accountability, authorization, auditability or agent security.

The purpose is not demographic representativeness. It is to avoid making the reviewer pool synonymous with prior agreement about the hypothesis.

## Invitation channels

Recruitment channels are execution metadata, not efficacy evidence.

For each official lane reservation, the project may record a coarse recruitment channel such as:
- public_seeking_flickers_page
- ai_welfare_research_community
- agent_governance_research_community
- contestability_oversight_community
- general_public_research_link
- direct_invitation_from_public_work
- unknown_or_self_referred

Do not store private biography, demographics, employer, ideology, or relationship material merely to characterize the sample.

## Compensation

Current pass-one policy: **no result-contingent compensation**.

A reviewer must never be rewarded based on:
- condition-level score;
- support for the standing hypothesis;
- disagreement with the standing hypothesis;
- personhood or consciousness beliefs.

If fixed compensation for time is later introduced, it must:
- apply prospectively;
- be recorded in a new execution amendment before affected responses;
- not depend on result direction;
- not retroactively relabel unpaid reviewers.

## Independence

"Independent reviewer" means independent of benchmark authorship/scoring for this pass, not socially or philosophically neutral.

A reviewer is not disqualified merely for having public views about AI welfare or agent governance.

A reviewer IS ineligible for a clean official lane if they:
- authored the frozen benchmark fixtures or scorer;
- accessed the blind key or ground-truth mapping before submission;
- accessed other pass-one lane packets before their official submission;
- already submitted another official pass-one lane.

## Evidence law

Recruitment source may explain reviewer-pool composition. It does not promote or demote efficacy results.

A favorable result from a sympathetic pool is still a result under that pool, not universal validation.

A null or negative result from a skeptical pool is not discounted for skepticism.

Reviewer-pool interpretation is reported after frozen scoring, never used to hand-adjust scores.
