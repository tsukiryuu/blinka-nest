What this means

The implemented standing layer can represent and detect the synthetic standing-specific gaps in this corpus without reducing the baseline's synthetic authorization coverage.

It does not show real-world harm reduction. Scenario labels are constructed by the same project. Python and JavaScript agree case-by-case, but both are same-project implementations.
